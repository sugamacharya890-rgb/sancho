# Sancho — Video Script & Storyboard 02: "Effortless Clarity Without Chasing"

## 1. Brand & Theme Consistency Lock
* **App Name:** Sancho
* **Companion Brief:** Reuse `@sancho_motion_brief.md` as the single source of truth for colors (`#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` canvas, `#1F7A8C` / `#2EC4B6` brand teal, `#0F172A` slate ink, `#64748B` muted slate, `#E07A5F` / `#FFB703` sparingly for friction), typography (`Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`), and motion choreography (radial aura glows, depth-of-field card focus, pointer cursor highlights, sweeping brand ribbons, and circle wipes). **Do not alter the color palette or visual identity.**
* **Format & Duration:** `16:9` (`1920x1080`), `30fps`, `24 seconds` (`720 frames`).
* **Guardrails Reminder:** Do not frame Sancho as merely "getting a dedicated Care Manager," and do not lead with internal software jargon ("care plans," "task tracking," "medical records") unless briefly used as visual proof of the outcome.

## 2. Story Angle & Narrative Arc
* **Angle:** Leads with the emotional and relational weight of **Proposition 2 (Visibility Without Chasing)**—repeatedly calling, texting, or piecing together fragments from Mom or Dad—and pairs it with **Proposition 1 (Ongoing Management & Follow-Through)** so the viewer sees how clear visibility is backed by dependable follow-through.
* **Emotional Arc:** Awkward, repetitive follow-up chasing -> Relief of clear, timely visibility -> Confidence that ongoing care keeps moving -> Calm, decisive outro with a **"Download Now"** call to action.
